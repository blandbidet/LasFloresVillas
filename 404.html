The Island

Discover St. John

St. John is the smallest and most enchanting of the US Virgin Islands, a place where two-thirds of the island is a protected National Park.

  • 🏖
    World-Class BeachesTrunk Bay, Oppenheimer Beach, and more — all within a short drive
  • 🌿
    Virgin Islands National Park14,737 acres of protected tropical forest, hiking trails, and pristine coral reefs
  • 🌊
    SnorkelingCrystal-clear Caribbean water with vibrant reefs teeming with marine life
  • 🍽
    DiningCruz Bay's restaurants, bars, and spas are within walking distance
